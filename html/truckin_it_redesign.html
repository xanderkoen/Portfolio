Mijn Rol & Netwerk

Truckin' it! is onze nieuwe Multiplayer game die wij op Steam releasen. Het is een Chaotic Delivery simulator game met friends.

Tijdens dit project ben ik een developer geweest en heb ik mij in veel verschillende dingen verdiept. Ik en mijn team hebben ervoor gekozen een multiplayer game te maken dus heb ik mijzelf ook verdiept in Networking. De game is gebouwd in Unity met FishNet networking en Steamworks.NET.

About the Game

Truckin' it is a fast-paced online two-player co-op delivery game where you have to deliver your cargo as fast as possible while also making sure you don't lose any of the cargo.

Trust your friend and work together to achieve the fastest and most efficient delivery run. There are faster but riskier roads, but also longer and safer routes; pick whatever floats your truck.

Are you a risky player who likes to be surprised with different hazards and puts everything on the line for the best score? Or do you prefer to play it safe? Enjoy the silly environment and aesthetic while trying to navigate the chaos in the new upcoming hit game, Truckin' it.
